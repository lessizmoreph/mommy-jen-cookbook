About the Author

Jeannie Rebadulla

Pastor Woman of God Visionary Amazing Cook

Jeannie Rebadulla is a woman of faith, a dedicated pastor, and a visionary whose love for bringing people together through food has inspired generations. A gifted cook with roots deeply embedded in Filipino culture and its rich Spanish colonial heritage, Jeannie channels her warmth, creativity, and nurturing spirit into every dish she creates.

Her recipes are more than instructions — they are invitations to gather, to celebrate, and to pass on the treasured flavors of our heritage. Through Flavors of Heritage, she shares not just her culinary wisdom, but a piece of her heart, her faith, and her profound belief that food is one of God’s most beautiful gifts to humanity.

“Cook with love, serve with joy, and gather often.”
